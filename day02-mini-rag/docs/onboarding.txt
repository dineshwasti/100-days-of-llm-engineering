New Employee Onboarding Guide

Before your first day, IT ships your laptop to your home address. It usually
arrives 2-3 business days before your start date. Do not open it until day one.

On your first morning, you'll join a welcome call at 9:30 AM with your manager
and the People team. IT will help you set up your accounts (email, Slack,
GitHub) during this call. Make sure your laptop is charged.

Your first week is focused on orientation: company overview, meeting your
team, and setting up your local development environment. You are not expected
to ship any code in week one.

By the end of week two, you should have completed the "Getting Started" guide
in the internal wiki and made your first small pull request, even if it's just
a documentation fix.
