Employee Benefits Summary

Health insurance coverage begins on your first day of employment, with no
waiting period. The company covers 100% of the premium for the employee and
80% for dependents.

Employees get 20 days of paid time off per year, plus all public holidays.
Unused PTO can roll over up to 5 days into the next year.

The company offers a learning and development stipend of $1,000 per year,
which can be used for courses, books, or conference tickets. Requests are
approved by your manager.

Remote employees receive a one-time $500 home office setup allowance during
their first month.
